This update sharpens the wallet and steadies the interpreter.

• More dependable payments. The wallet now correctly accounts for coins already spent elsewhere, so transactions build and broadcast cleanly the first time.
• Paymail payments notify the recipient's wallet automatically.
• Files saved on another device now download promptly — the app asks iCloud for them instead of waiting.
• A steadier interpreter — several edge cases that could interrupt a session are now handled gracefully, and large transactions are faster.
• Refreshed Settings, and a smoother wallet loading animation.
