Design spec · 2026-08-23 · henceforth.club · redesign proposal, awaiting Henry's go

Phthalo: the club gets its own colour

Two asks from Henry — use phthalo green more, and put the apps at the top instead of behind an Apps button — plus a full read of the live site. The finding underneath both: the club has been borrowing Deck of Cards' cyan as its own brand, and the hero hides its most important text behind a JavaScript animation. Phthalo fixes the first; deleting one wrapper fixes the second.

The two wounds. First: henceforth.club answers with a 307 redirect to www.henceforth.club — about 775 ms of modelled first-visit delay on mobile — while metadataBase, the sitemap, and every printed link declare the bare domain as canonical. The site pays a round-trip to contradict its own metadata. Second: the hero copy is wrapped in the Motion FadeIn component, which renders it at opacity 0 until JavaScript loads, hydrates, and animates. The largest contentful paint element is that paragraph — the site's most important sentence is the last thing to exist. First paint happens at 1.9 s; the words arrive at 4.0 s.

The brand finding. Cyan #5eead4 is doing two jobs: it is Deck of Cards' identity and the club's wordmark, glow, selection colour, hero wash, and grid. Henceforth has amber, Hansard has green, Folklore has orange — the club itself has nothing of its own. That is the real reason "use phthalo green more" is the right instinct and not just a repaint.

2 · Phthalo: the palette

Proposal. Phthalo green becomes the club's own material at two depths: the pigment itself (#123524, the blue-shade phthalocyanine painters glaze shadows with) as the ground — every near-black surface shifts from blue-black to phthalo-black — and a luminous viridian as the club's accent for the wordmark, links, selection, and chrome. The four app accents do not move an inch: amber stays Henceforth, cyan goes back to being only Deck's, green stays Hansard, orange stays Folklore. The club stops borrowing.

Where the pigment goes to work (the "use it more" of the ask): the hero's radial washes swap cyan for phthalo glazes — rgba(18,53,36,…) layered at two radii, the way the pigment is actually used on canvas; the hero grid lines go phthalo; the terminal window's outer glow goes phthalo; ::selection goes club-accent at 22%; the section-line gradients pick up --card-border's new green automatically. Because every surface already reads tokens, the whole shift is roughly twenty lines in :root plus the handful of hard-coded cyan rgba values in globals.css (.glow-cyan, .hero-gradient, .hero-grid, .terminal-window, ::selection, input focus). The scoped rooms — paper articles, Folklore's black, Provenance's white — re-declare their own tokens and are untouched by design.

Type does not move. Space Mono everywhere is the identity; this redesign spends nothing there.

3 · The shelf: apps at the top

Today the desktop nav hides the three store apps behind a hover-gated Apps button — the menu is conditionally rendered, so the product names are not even in the server markup of most pages — while a generic word takes the slot the products should own. The mobile menu already lists them flat, which is the right answer.

Before   henceforth.club     Apps ▾   Learn  Docs  Articles  folklore  Contact  (Sign in)
                              └─ Henceforth / Deck of Cards / Hansard   · hover only

After    henceforth.club     ▎Henceforth  ▎Deck of Cards  ▎Hansard  ▎folklore  ·  Learn  Docs  Articles  Sign in
  • Every app is a top-level link. Each carries a two-pixel tick in its own accent colour — the nav becomes the app index, and the accent system the site already owns does the labelling. Hover colours stay exactly as the dropdown had them.
  • Contact leaves the top bar (it is three X links; the footer already carries it). That funds the width the app names need.
  • The hamburger breakpoint moves from sm to md — below that the inline shelf will not fit honestly. If the full "Deck of Cards" crowds the medium widths, it shortens to "Deck" at md and speaks fully from lg.
  • The dropdown machinery is deleted — appsOpen state, hover handlers, conditional menu — the Navbar gets simpler, and app links are in the server markup of every page.
  • The sign-in pill quiets down. It is the operator's door, yet today it is the loudest element in the chrome for every visitor. It becomes a muted ghost link that only glows once signed in.

What is already right and should be left alone: first byte at ~70 ms, zero layout shift, 40 ms blocking time, subset fonts preloaded with swap, a 16.6 KB stylesheet, no hero imagery to weigh down paint. The site's problem was never the server or the assets — it is one redirect and one opacity:0.

5 · Ease of use and accessibility

  • Contrast floor. Every failing element is a text-muted/40..60 opacity fraction on near-black — footer link columns, taglines, section eyebrows. The two-tier muted ramp (§2) replaces fractions with real colours that clear 4.5:1; the footer's easter-egg whispers may stay whispers, but nudged to the floor so the lore is legible to everyone.
  • List semantics. The homepage ladder renders <ol> → <div> (FadeIn) → <li> — invalid, and flagged. FadeIn moves inside the <li> (or renders as one) and the failure disappears along with Motion.
  • Copy drift. "Three apps. One dream." sits above four products, and the shared metadata says "Three apps, one dream" on every card. Drop the count rather than maintain it: the description already carries the three-clause poem, which needs no number. Whether Folklore joins the headline ("FORTH. Cards. Parliament.") is Henry's call under the no-announcements-before-launch gate — the card and nav presence it already has stays either way.
  • The ladder stays. "Start here" 1–4 is numbered because it genuinely is a sequence — that is structure carrying information, keep it.
  • Golden-ratio cards, terminal hero, times-table circle, all four eggs: keep. They are the memorable furniture; the redesign changes the light in the room, not the room.

6 · What deliberately does not change

Space Mono. The Phosphor Noir posture. The per-app accent system. The scoped rooms (paper, Folklore black, Provenance). The board world. The Folklore announcement gate. No new dependencies — this spec only deletes one.
