Phthalo green — the mock-ups

henceforth.club · 24 August 2026 · the Home Alone green · companion to the redesign spec

Two full renderings of the homepage: the site as it is live today (the before), then the proposed phthalo livery (the after) — cream wordmark on deep blue-green ground, and a shelf that carries only the apps, folklore, Contact, and Sign in — nothing else. Learn, Docs, and Articles fold into the Henceforth section, where they already belong.

Steer (Henry, 24 August): the green is the one in the pigment dish — phthalo, the Home Alone green — deeper and bluer than the warm racing-green cast of the first cut. The roundel cream carries over: it reads even better on the colder green. And the shelf loses Learn, Docs, and Articles — they are Henceforth content and move inside it.

5 · Should we go live?

My verdict: yes. Every change is presentation-layer — colour tokens, the navigation bar, and how the hero enters. No money paths, no board machinery, no data. A push deploys in about thirty seconds, and a plain revert undoes the whole thing just as fast.

  • What ships: the phthalo tokens and cyan sweep; the shelf carrying only Henceforth, Deck of Cards, Hansard, folklore, Contact, and a quiet Sign in; Learn, Docs, and Articles folded into the Henceforth section; the hero painted without waiting for JavaScript; and the accessibility fixes (muted-text contrast floor, real list markup).
  • What it buys: the club finally owns a colour; the apps are one click from everywhere; largest contentful paint should drop from about 4.0 seconds toward the 2.2-second target.
  • The one thing I cannot push: making the bare domain primary (the apex redirect) lives in the Vercel dashboard — worth doing, not blocking, can follow separately.
  • The one judgement call left: cream next to Henceforth's amber in the shelf — neighbours, not twins; judge it in mock-up two above. The colder phthalo ground makes the amber pop harder than the racing-green cut did.
