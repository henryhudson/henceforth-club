Design spec · 2026-08-25 · henceforth.club/folklore · status: DRAFT for Henry's review

One board, Elo

Twetch and Treechat posts are already on Bitcoin. Folklore ranks them with the duel Elo that already exists: two posts, tap one. The work is a mapper into that pool, not a new ranking formula.

What exists. Folklore already has Elo: an append-only duel ledger (kudos:duels), a pure fold (applyDuel / foldTable), start rating 1500, K 40 until twenty duels then 16, author rating the mean of their top five texts. The dealer puts two posts on the table; a tap is one duel; sortPostsByElo is the board. The arena candidate pool today is the first page of fifty registered X-archive handles, via Redis. The launchd worker is dead. The web archive flag is dark. Twetch.com shut in June 2024; the posts remain as Magic Attribute Protocol records, app=twetch type=post. Treechat writes the same Bitcoin Schema. Henry, 2026-08-25, on ranking: use the Elo system.

The ask. Henry, 2026-08-25: “it would be good we need the data from twetch and treechat ideally so we can rank them.” And the standing lean rule from the same morning: Bitcoin and the mini, no Redis poller.

Decision 1 — rank is Elo from folklore duels, not satoshis and not Twetch/Treechat likes. The existing fold is the ranking: two posts dealt, one tap, applyDuel. A text nobody has dueled sits at 1500. Twetch likes and Treechat upvalue are cardinal payments, not pairwise outcomes — they must not be synthesised into fake duels. They may still show as “earned on their own chain” on a card; they do not move the Elo table. Importing a post puts it in the dealer's candidate pool. Ranking starts when someone taps.
Decision 2 — point, do not copy. Each post's identity is its original transaction id. Folklore does not re-inscribe Twetch or Treechat text, and does not copy media into a blob store. The reader resolves media from the source transaction (B protocol / ordinal outpoint). Re-wrapping as SocialArchive is allowed later as an optional bundle; it is not required to rank.
Decision 3 — no daemon. The retired 15-second worker stays dead. The mini may run a once-a-day settlement job, and nothing that ticks in between.
Decision 4 — people submit their own links; Elo ranks; kudos is the money; the mini pays once a day. Henry, 2026-08-25: “we want people to submit their own links and hope people vote on them. then we can send them the money at the end of each day.” Do not scrape Twetch or Treechat. A submitter pastes a URL (a Twetch post, a Treechat post, anything https). The feed chip is the source (Twetch / Treechat / the rest). Voting is the existing duel: two cards, tap one, Elo moves. The tap spends kudos from the voter's float; that kudos accrues as earned on the winning submitter. Elo does not mint money — a high rating with no taps pays nothing. Once a day the mini pays each handle's unsettled kudos as bitcoin to the bound address, then markSettled. No 15-second custody loop: the submitter pays their own inscription (app wallet, or the smallest web quote), and the payout is one batch transaction.

1 · One post, three apps

Bitcoin Schema already is the shared shape. A post on chain looks like:

OP_RETURN
  B <text or file> <media-type> utf-8
  | MAP SET app twetch|treechat type post [paymail …]
  | AIP BITCOIN_ECDSA <address> <signature>

Folklore's own archives are a fourth encoding (SocialArchive JSON) of the same idea: handle, posts, media outpoints. The mapper's job is to turn any of these into one in-memory post:

{ id: txid,          // original transaction, never a new one
  source: "twetch" | "treechat" | "x" | "folklore",
  at, text,
  author: aipAddress | paymail | handle,
  media: [{ outpoint, kind }],
  parentTxid? }

SocialArchive.source is already a free string. No wire change for wrapping. The Elo table is keyed by postId. For a MAP post that id is the original transaction id, which is also DealCandidate.postId.

2 · The pool, not a new ranker

Today listPoolTexts only sees registered X-archive handles. Twetch and Treechat posts join that list as ordinary candidates:

{ postId: twetchOrTreechatTxid,
  author: aipAddress | paymail,
  priority: 0 }

The dealer, the K schedule, the unranked badge, and sortPostsByElo do not change. A Twetch post dueled against a Treechat post is a legal pair: different authors whenever the pool allows, same-author only when it cannot. The arena eligibility rule already accepts text or media (isArenaEligible).

The duel ledger is append-only and the rating table is a replay. That ledger currently lives in Redis. It is a short list of pairs, not a KEYS scan, and it is not the retired worker. Until an inscribed duel log exists, that small Redis list may stay; it is not a reason to stand the worker back up. A later slice can write each resolved duel as a Bitcoin Schema record (or a daily JSONL on the mini) and fold from that instead.

3 · Ingest

Mapper (pure). Magic Attribute Protocol bytes in, a pool text out, or null. One fixture: a real Twetch post transaction. One fixture: a real Treechat post transaction. No network in the mapper. Likes and upvalue are out of scope for the mapper — they are not duels.

Twetch scan (finite). On the mini, once: walk JungleBus / WhatsOnChain history for app=twetch type=post. Write a local JSONL of mapped pool texts (gitignored, like the board). Twetch will not grow.

Treechat scan. Same mapper, filter app to Treechat's published app name (pin from a live post before coding — do not guess). First pass can be Henry's own addresses plus a named list (the Craig Wright seed card is this list).

Show. Folklore feed and arena read the JSONL (or an inscribed manifest of transaction ids) as extra DealCandidates beside the X-archive pool. Sort with sortPostsByElo. Undueled imports sit at 1500 until the first tap.

4 · Identity

Twetch users are paymails / usernames in MAP, signed by an AIP address from the Twetch seed family Henceforth already derives (m/0/0 and payment chains). Treechat users are the same schema. Folklore users are bound X handles. Do not invent a new identity. Display whatever MAP carries; ranking does not need a handle at all (it keys on post transaction id).

Henry's own Twetch archive is the first ingest: the app already knows the address family. Other people (Craig Wright) need a named address or paymail on the seed list, not an X API pull.

5 · What this is not

  • Not a live Twetch clone, not a Treechat client, not a JungleBus subscription that ticks forever.
  • Not a new ranker, not a conversion of likes into fake duels, not the retired web-archive worker.
  • Not Hodlocker in version one, though it is the same protocol and can ride the mapper later.
  • Not re-hosting video. Original outpoints or it does not show.

6 · Open questions

  • Q1. First ingest: Henry's Twetch only, or the named seed list (Craig Wright and friends) in the same pass?
  • Q2. Treechat live updates: on-demand lookup when someone opens folklore, or a daily mini scan?
  • Q3. Pin one live Treechat post as a mapper fixture — Henry or a public Treechat URL. (Upvalue is display-only; not needed for Elo.)
  • Q4. Arena access: existing kudos-float gate, or the 27 July front-door “two posts, dealt” for anyone? Ranking cannot move until someone is allowed to tap.

No money path in this spec. The mapper is pure. Ingest is a scan. Ranking is the existing Elo fold over folklore duels.
