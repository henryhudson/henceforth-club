The 29 July triple ship, seen

Wednesday ships all three apps — Deck 1.27 (full localization), Henceforth 4.49/4.50 (the money fixes), Hansard 1.5 (This Week front door). This plan wraps that ship in every visibility lever the 26 July research pass found worth pulling: ratings prompts riding the builds, the store metadata fixed before submit, and a launch surround of the named channels where each app's audience demonstrably lives. Grounded in the three research briefs; honest about ceilings throughout.

Three constraints that outrank visibility. (1) The Henceforth money fix does not wait for marketing — if the 4.49 archive happens before the review prompt lands, the prompt rides the next cycle. (2) Apple allows the system rating prompt three times per user per year: every prompt fires only at a genuinely earned completion moment, never at launch, never as a tap-reaction, and never gated on predicted sentiment (gating violates Apple's rules). (3) Everything Henry-voiced — X posts, pitches, the Product Hunt launch — is drafted here but posted only by Henry; content stays review-gated.
