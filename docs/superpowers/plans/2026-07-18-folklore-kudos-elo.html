Kudos — one-on-one ranking, a proper Elo

Kudos are satoshis wearing a friendlier name. The arena deals two texts; you give kudos — one or many — to the one you prefer, and that gesture crowns the duel: 0.1 pence per kudos moves from your float to the winning author, and the rating that sorts the whole folklore is a pure fold over the duels. One document — the design and the build plan together, at Henry's request.

Decisions settled in brainstorm (Henry, 2026-07-18): kudos are paid but never called satoshis in copy · duels replace the planned up/down paid-vote pipeline (xtext-v2-t4/t5 superseded) · one site-wide Elo pool · a kudos is 0.1 UK pence · opening a profile costs £2 + the archive's inscription cost and the £2 returns to the user as their kudos float (~2,000 kudos) · giving kudos is click-only, no QR at the moment of choice · kudos are private · in-feed kudos on any text as you browse · no vote buttons anywhere — the kudos gesture itself (one or more) crowns the dealt pair's winner; the amount is money and generosity, the rating swing stays binary (2026-07-18 evening correction) · Stripe card payments alongside the bitcoin QR for profile creation, both crediting the same float ledger; the card price grosses up to cover fees and a tax reserve — the tax numbers themselves are set with Henry in the taxes session on 2026-07-19 · late revision (Henry, 2026-07-18 night): the kudos surfaces become two pages plus the recent feed — the dealt stream is the default page, the Best tab and author directory die, and a daily Top chart ranks by kudos received that day with self-reply threads rolling up to their root (threads of work chart as one unit, continuation posts shown first) · kudosing your own work never counts and is refused · profiles stay archival — no following, no social layer · native visitor comments deferred to their own design pass.

Part one — the design

1 · Product shape and money flow

Opening a folklore profile is repriced from the staged flat £1 to £2 + inscription cost, priced at the live pound rate through the existing quoteArchive sum type (fails closed on no rate, floors above fee + dust). The £2 is not revenue: it funds the buyer's kudos float — 2,000 kudos at 0.1 pence each, a private balance attached to their profile. Kudos spent flow to the receiving text's author; authors' earnings accrue privately and the Mac mini worker settles them on-chain in real satoshis on a daily aggregated sweep. The economy is circular: your £2 funds your taste-making, being ranked well funds more of yours.

Two funding rails, one float ledger. Bitcoiners pay the existing QR; everyone else pays card via Stripe Checkout — the webhook credits the float as closed-loop kudos points and starts the archive job. On the card rail no bitcoin moves at funding time at all: the payer's float is ledger points spendable only in the folklore, and treasury converts to satoshis only when authors settle. That closed-loop framing is deliberate Stripe posture — the card payer never receives withdrawable cryptocurrency (the authors do, and they are not the card payers), which keeps the product out of Stripe's restricted-business category. Consequence: the withdraw-your-float fast follow stays parked while card payments exist.

Pricing grosses up, in one place: card_price = (FLOAT_POUNDS + inscription_cost + MARGIN_POUNDS) / (1 − TAX_RESERVE_RATE) + stripe_fees, every parameter a named constant. TAX_RESERVE_RATE is a placeholder until the taxes session (2026-07-19, with Henry's accountant's numbers — value-added-tax exposure, capital-gains treatment of treasury disposals, and the income reserve are decided there, not here). A revenue ledger records every sale (gross, Stripe fee, tax reserve, float liability, inscription cost) and every settlement's market value at time of send (each treasury disposal is a capital-gains event), exportable as a spreadsheet for the accountant.

Custody, named honestly: between funding and settlement the float pool is platform custody — a step past spec B's ephemeral per-job class, because it stands. It therefore ships flag-gated behind a full adversarial money-path review (task 11) and a funded end-to-end (task 12).

2 · The Elo algorithm — a pure fold, never a stored number

Exactly the xScore.ts shape: an append-only duel ledger is the only truth; ratings are a deterministic replay over it, so a correction (a gamed batch struck out, a refunded float) is a replay, never a patch to a cached number.

  • Ledger entry: { seq, duelId, winnerPostId, loserPostId, day } — no giver identity in the fold's input. Privacy is structural, not policy.
  • Update: R′ = R + K·(1 − E) for the winner, mirrored for the loser, with E = 1/(1 + 10^((R_loser − R_winner)/400)). All texts start at 1500.
  • K schedule: K=40 while a text has fewer than 20 duels (provisional — finds its level fast, shows an "unranked" badge), K=16 after (the top stays stable).
  • Author rating ("kudos to users"): mean of the author's top five texts — rewards peaks, not flooding.
  • Reads: a cached fold checkpoint (ratings as of sequence N) plus a fold of duels after N. The checkpoint is a cache, never a source of truth.

3 · Surfaces

Three surfaces, one job each (late revision, 2026-07-18 night — the arena-as-destination and the Best/directory surfaces die; two pages plus the feed that already exists):

The default page (/folklore): the dealt stream — the landing surface is the arena. The server deals texts on the committed mix (good Elo plus wildcards; the precise arms in §4), each carrying the same kudos control with no vote buttons: the first kudos on either text of the dealt pair crowns it (binary, whatever the amount), further taps in the same beat are generosity to the winner (tap = one, keep tapping or hold = more — all of it to the author), kudos on an already-resolved pair degrade to tips, and the next pair deals instantly. Visitors without a float see the controls disabled with the one-line "open your archive to get kudos" nudge — the £2 funnel.

The top page (/folklore/top): the daily chart. Texts rank by kudos received that day — today by default, past days navigable (the append-only ledgers reconstruct any date). The chart stays pure: no wildcards injected — discovery is the dealt stream's job (its provisional and random arms) and the recent feed's; the daily reset itself fights entrenchment, since yesterday's winner starts today at zero. Elo keeps deciding what gets dealt but is no longer a headline anywhere. Threads roll up: a kudos landing anywhere in a self-reply thread counts toward the thread root's chart score — threads of work chart as one unit, encouraged deliberately — while the money still goes to whoever was kudos'd. A charting thread renders as a unit, the author's continuation posts shown first.

The recent feed (chronological, already live): the completeness guarantee — every text appears the moment it is archived, no algorithm deciding anything; one tap from the default page. In-feed kudos: every text row and single-post page keeps the identical control — tips, not duels: they never move Elo (a proper Elo cannot ingest single-sided applause); they raise the text's dealing priority, buying it duel exposure it must then win on merit. One gesture everywhere; only the dealt pair turns it into a ranking event.

What dies: the Best tab and the author directory (built in task 9 behind the flag, never seen in production — re-cut by task 9R). authorRatings stays in the core as tested pure code; nothing renders it. Profiles remain archival artifacts — no following, no social layer. Native visitor comments (living conversations on archived posts) are deferred to their own design pass — identity, storage, and moderation questions, not a scoring rule.

4 · The dealer and anti-gaming

Pairs are server-dealt, never chosen: 60% rating-adjacent (informative duels), 20% provisional-versus-established (fast placement), 15% tip-priority, 5% uniform random (nothing rots undealt). A duel resolution is valid only with the dealer's dealt-pair token — single use, ten-minute expiry, bound to the session — so a matchup cannot be constructed by the voter, which kills targeted self-pumping. Remaining self-kudos economics: you cannot pick your pairing, Elo's expected-score term makes repeat wins over weak opposition worth less and less, and a per-float daily duel cap (200) bounds damage. Self-kudos are refused at the route (payer's handle matching the receiving text's — or its thread root's — author): paying yourself serves no honest purpose, and with thread roll-up it would let an author buy chart position through their own continuation posts; one refusal covers both. The residual sybil — a second £2 profile pumping the first — is priced as what it is: paid promotion at full cost, every unit pre-paid float, append-only logged; it goes on the money lens of the adversarial review (task 11). The strike-and-replay corrections lever covers whatever slips through.

5 · Data model and settlement

KeyHoldsVisibility

kudos:tips:<postId>public tip count; giver never recordedcount public
kudos:earned:<handle>accrued kudos awaiting settlementprivate
kudos:fold:checkpointratings as of sequence N (cache)internal
kudos:pair:<token>dealt pair, single-use, 10-minute expiryinternal

Conservation invariant (tested, load-bearing): for every profile and globally, funded = float + spent and spent = earned_unsettled + settled, all in integer kudos units. A kudos is never minted, never double-spent, never lost to rounding — conversion to satoshis happens only at the two edges (funding in, settlement out, each at that day's live rate; the platform bears drift between them, small at these sizes). Settlement: a daily worker sweep pays each author's accrual in one aggregated transaction — no giver identities on-chain — dust-floored at 546 satoshis with sub-dust rolling to the next sweep, logged like every worker money movement.

Float identity: profile creation issues an opaque bearer token — set as a cookie and shown once as a recovery string. Another device means pasting the recovery string. Deliberately primitive for v1; account recovery beyond the string is out of scope.
