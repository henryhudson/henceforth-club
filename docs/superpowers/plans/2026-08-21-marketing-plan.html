The reach plan

The apps are good and almost nobody knows. This plan drives downloads and Deck subscriptions with zero spend, by attaching one marketing act to each rhythm the week already has — and it is measured every morning in the report, so it cannot quietly stop happening.

The ground truth this plan starts from (21 August). Deck: forty-seven downloads a week from generic store search, eight paying subscribers (four monthly, four yearly) plus six free trials, 4.4 stars on seven ratings. Henceforth: five stars on two ratings, zero recorded downloads, subtitle and keywords staged for Wednesday. The Hansard: five stars on one rating, zero recorded downloads — and Parliament is in summer recess, so its zeros are seasonal as much as anything. The site: ten views a day. The one proven channel: Henry's own X posts, weekly, for Sci Fri.
Three standing rules. No hype vocabulary anywhere — the tone of the digest is the tone of the marketing. No paid acquisition — nothing here costs money. Henry posts everything himself — posting is the sign-off, exactly as it is for the films.

The one-line theory

Deck proves the store machinery works: when people search words that exist ("deck of cards"), downloads follow. Henceforth and Hansard have words nobody searches, so their reach must come from artifacts people share — and the operation already produces shareable artifacts every single week (a film, an episode, a digest, a daily inscribed newspaper). The plan is to point that existing production line at the store, not to build a new one.

The four workstreams

Workstream A · Deck of Cards — the paying product

Convert the trials, sharpen the loop

  • Trial conversion. Six people are in free trial today. A paywall copy pass on the subscription view — benefit-led ("play any card game with anyone, anywhere"), yearly anchored against monthly — is a one-Wednesday craft with a direct line to revenue.
  • The loop is the product. Multiplayer requires inviting a friend; every match is an acquisition event. Audit the invite path (start match → friend installs) for friction, and make "play with a friend" the first line of the listing description.
  • Ratings volume. Seven ratings is thin for ranking. Verify the rating prompt fires at the happy moment (after a finished match). Target: twenty-five ratings by mid-October, asked honestly, never nagged.
  • Season. November and December are card-game season. A festive listing-screenshot refresh and a "games night" post series are prepared in late October, not improvised in December.
Workstream B · The Hansard — the September play

Be ready the day the House returns

  • Wednesday 26 August: 1.9 ships the staged keywords and the digest description sentence. August numbers are not the test; September's are.
  • The living article (Henry's ask, 21 August, carded). This Week builds daily through the sitting week — questions, divisions and news landing on the page as they happen in Parliament, the draft visibly growing until Wednesday publication. Fresh content daily is the reason to visit, and each day's growth is a postable moment.
  • Post the digest. From the House's return, the Wednesday digest is posted on X the way the Friday film is — same ritual, same sign-off. On big vote days, one genuinely useful, rules-respecting contribution to the United Kingdom politics subreddit; value first, link second.
  • The calendar is the campaign. The return of the House, conference season, and the Budget are the traffic spikes; each gets a planned presence, not a reaction.
Workstream C · Henceforth — the launch play

One launch, done properly, to exactly the right room

  • Wednesday 26 August: the subtitle ("Programmable Bitcoin wallet") and keywords go live with 4.53.
  • Show Hacker News (early September, once 4.53 settles): "a Forth terminal that is also a Bitcoin wallet" is precisely that audience's kind of object. Preparation: a ninety-second demo capture, the henceforth.club/henceforth page as the landing, and a cleared morning-to-evening answer window. One shot; the product page and the /learn course do the rest.
  • The drumbeat continues: Thinking Henceforth every Sunday, already staged ahead.
  • The folklore showroom joins the plan only when Henry lifts his own launch gate — it is not marketed before it is ready.
Workstream D · The production line — turn the working channel into a funnel

The films, the paper, the site

  • One standing line under every Friday film post: a link to henceforth.club. The channel that works gains a funnel without changing its character.
  • The physics app is the season's launch (after the Maxwell finale, 16 October): ten films become the trailer library for the Feynman Figures launch — the one moment this year with real launch-day material.
  • The paper is live at /board/report already (it re-reads the board on every load) and every edition is inscribed on-chain — an artifact strange and honest enough to be worth pointing at, occasionally, in its own right.
