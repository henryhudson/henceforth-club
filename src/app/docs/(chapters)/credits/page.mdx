## SwiftBSV

The Swift SDK that powers every cryptographic operation in the Henceforth wallet — BIP-32/39 key derivation, Type42 (BRC-42) invoice keys, ECDSA signing, BSM, ECIES (BIE1 wire format), Script construction, the full BSV opcode set, transaction building, and SPV verification. Originally written by Will Townsend at [wtsnz/SwiftBSV](https://github.com/wtsnz/SwiftBSV); the fork at [henryhudson/SwiftBSV](https://github.com/henryhudson/SwiftBSV) continues that foundation with Swift 6 strict concurrency, modern Apple platform targets, and a 41-page LaTeX book of its own.

[SwiftBSV Book (PDF)](https://github.com/henryhudson/SwiftBSV/blob/main/Documentation/swiftbsv.pdf)
